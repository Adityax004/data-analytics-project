# Business Findings and Recommendations

The pipeline writes data-backed findings to `outputs/tables/business_insights.json`. Use this document as the executive narrative shell for presentation or portfolio publishing.

## Findings to Review

1. Highest revenue customer segment
2. At-risk and lost customers with high historical value
3. Peak sales months and seasonality
4. Most profitable product categories
5. Discount buckets with margin pressure
6. Top revenue and retention regions
7. Month-one cohort retention behavior

## Recommended Actions

- Build loyalty and early-access journeys for Champions and Loyal Customers.
- Launch second-purchase offers inside the first 30 days after acquisition.
- Suppress broad discounts where profit margin is weak; target discounts to At Risk customers.
- Use high-profit categories as anchors for bundles and cross-sells.
- Localize retention campaigns in regions with strong revenue but below-average repeat rate.
- Monitor monthly cohort retention after campaign launches.
